      How to play

      Drag a piece, or click it and then click where it should go. Dots mark the legal moves. You can also type moves such as Nf3, O-O or e7e8q in the box under the move list.

      Right-click and drag to draw arrows; right-click a square to circle it. Hold Shift, Alt or Ctrl for red, blue or yellow. A left click clears them.
